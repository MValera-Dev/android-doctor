# Android Doctor

Finds why your Unity Android build fails or gets rejected by Google Play, and fixes the common causes with one click.

Open it with **Window > Android Doctor** and press **Check project**. It looks at your JDK/SDK/NDK/Gradle setup, your Player Settings, every native library for the 16 KB page-size requirement and, if your last build failed in Gradle, the reason. Then **Fix all** applies every change that needs no decision from you, after listing it and asking.

Nothing runs until you press a button, and files it edits are backed up first.

Full documentation, what each button does, known limitations and how to report a problem with **Copy report**: https://github.com/MValera-Dev/android-doctor
